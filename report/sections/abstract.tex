We examine whether estimating asset-specific uncertainty improves portfolio decisions. Replicating enhanced portfolio optimization raises the gross Sharpe ratio from 0.32 to 0.77. In simulations with known uncertainty, scalar adjustment loses 12--29\% of oracle utility. Heterogeneous adjustment retains gains at the error dispersion measured in synthetic calibration. Historically, a common volatility target and gross cap yield incremental annual net utility of +18 bp in 1970--2018 and $-44$ bp in 2019--2025, with both baseline confidence intervals including zero. Higher trading costs reduce the early gain. The historical evidence supports scalar adjustment as a practical baseline, with no consistent net improvement from asset-specific uncertainty.
