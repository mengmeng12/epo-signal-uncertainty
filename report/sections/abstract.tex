We examine when a single uncertainty adjustment preserves portfolio decisions. A replication of enhanced portfolio optimization recovers its improvement over unregularized allocation. Simulations with known signal uncertainty isolate losses that remain after choosing the best scalar adjustment. These losses rise with heterogeneity and its overlap with portfolio exposures. We then estimate relative uncertainty from bootstrap forecasts and compare matched industry portfolios. Annual net certainty equivalent differences are 28 basis points in 1970--2018 and negative 99 basis points in 2019--2025; both confidence intervals include zero. Higher average returns are offset by additional risk and costs in the feasible heterogeneous strategy.
